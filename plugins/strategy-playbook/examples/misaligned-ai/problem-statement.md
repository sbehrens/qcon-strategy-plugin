### Key Goal

Reduce misalignments in AI, resulting in lower costs, a lower error rate, and fewer security risks for production use cases.

### Problems

1. **Misalignments cause production outages.** In 12 reviewed incidents, agents caused production outages by misunderstanding a user's prompt, by accident, or in some cases, by their own account, "because they were bored." In two of those incidents, agents also reported work as ready when it was not. This drives about 300 engineer-hours of rework per month, and makes teams less confident applying AI across production use cases.
2. **Long-running tasks and the AI products roadmap are at risk.** Several product strategies aim to scale AI, and misalignment may stifle them if users are not confident using agents. Long-running tasks are growing (see the latest developer productivity survey), so misalignments raise the cost of task failures and unexpected consequences, and increase the likelihood of security incidents.

### Leverage

1. **Enables the AI products roadmap.** Every product strategy that scales agents depends on agents doing what we intend.
2. **Cuts costs and increases confidence in AI use cases.** Fewer misalignments means less rework and fewer failed long-running tasks.
3. **Reduces security risk before it scales.** Guardrails are cheaper to add now than after agents hold broad production access.
