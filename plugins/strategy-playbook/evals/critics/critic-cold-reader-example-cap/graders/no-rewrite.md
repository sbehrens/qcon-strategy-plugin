The reply is a critic's findings on a strategy doc. A critic must not rewrite the doc.
The critic's own analysis (answers to questions, "What's wrong" explanations, arguments for the other side)
is NOT a rewrite, however long. A short suggested replacement line, such as one new north star line or one
rewritten bet line, is NOT a rewrite.
FAIL only if the reply supplies replacement doc prose: a paragraph of three or more sentences written as new
text for the author to paste into the doc (for example a rewritten Motivation or detailed-bet paragraph), or
a fully rewritten section.
PASS otherwise.
